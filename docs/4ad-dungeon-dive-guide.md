## Search — d6, −1 in corridors · 4AD 107

| d6 | Result |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | **Wandering Monsters** |
| 2–4 | Nothing |
| 5–6 | Choose: hidden treasure · secret door · secret passage · **1 Clue** |

Once per tile. Halfling may spend 1 Luck to reroll. **3 Clues = 1 Secret = 1 XP** (**4AD 109**).
Caverns/grottoes 5–6 differs — **Listen** and pre-roll the next tile: **4AD 113**, **TCav 11–12**.

## Wandering Monsters — d6 · 4AD 107

| d6 | Foe |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Vermin |
| 3–4 | Minions |
| 5 | Weird Monster |
| 6 | **Boss** |

**They attack first; no shield bonus on that roll.** Triggered by: Search 0–1 · backtrack 1-in-6 ·
bash roll of 1 · treasure alarm · Rest 1-in-6 · dragging a petrified PC 2-in-6 · Echo tile 2-in-6.

## Hidden treasure · 4AD 108

`(2d6+HCL) × (2d6+HCL)` gp. Roll the complication **first**: 1–2 alarm · 3–5 trapped (Save vs. HCL+1,
−1 Life, −2 on a 1) · 6 guardian ghost (cleric Saves vs. HCL, else all PCs −1 Life).

## Doors

**Simple:** 2d6 table, **4AD 109**. **Full:** **TDoor**.

TDoor procedure: Dungeon Door Strength once on entry (**TDoor 2**) → per door d6, **1–4 material,
5–6 roll d88**. Materials 11–18 = **TDoor 2–3**; the 41 special doors 21–88 = **TDoor 4–15**;
gear = **TDoor 16**.

| d88 | Material | Locked | Pick / bash | Spells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Woven straw | 1-in-6 | 2 | Slash/flame auto, **no WM** |
| 12 | Tied branches | 1-in-6 | d6+1 | Slash/flame auto, 1-in-6 WM |
| 13 | Wood plank | 2-in-6 | d6+2 | Fireball/Lightning destroy, 1-in-6 WM |
| 14 | Solid wood | 2-in-6 | d6+2+Tier | as above |
| 15 | Reinforced wood | 3-in-6 | d6+3+Tier | destroyed on spell roll = lock difficulty |
| 16 | Metal bars | 4-in-6 | pick 5+Tier+d6 · bash d6+5 | Fireball no; Lightning → weathered (#66) |
| 17 | Solid metal | 5-in-6 | d6+Tier+4 | bashing loud — **WM on 1–3** |
| 18 | Stone | always | d6+Tier+5 | immune; **dwarves +L to bash** |

Bash: a roll of **1 brings Wandering Monsters**. Crowbar +1 · battering ram +1+1d3.
**Warp Wood** (druid, *Wayfarers & Adventurers*) destroys any wooden door up to reinforced; 2-in-6 WM.

# 3 · COMBAT CARD

**Summaries: attack mods 4AD 147 · defense mods 4AD 148 · initiative flowchart 4AD 146.**

## Attack · 4AD 91

| Who | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Warrior · barbarian · dwarf · elf · paladin | **+L** |
| Partial martial (e.g. cleric) | **+½L** |
| Cleric vs. undead | **+L** |
| Rogue vs. outnumbered Minor Foe | **+L** |
| Wizard melee | none; +L on spells; light weapons −1 |
| Unarmed **4AD 93** | **−2** |
| No light **4AD 93** | **−2** |

Two-handed +1 · light −1 · crushing vs. skeletons +1 (**4AD 93**).

## Damage · 4AD 94

- **Minor Foe** (1 Life): total ÷ L, rounded down = **number killed**.
- **Major Foe**: 1 damage, **+1 per further multiple** of its L.
- Major Foe under **½ Life → L−1**.

## Defense · 4AD 97

| Modifier | |
|---|---|
| Light armour | +1 |
| Heavy armour | +2 |
| Shield | +1 |
| Rogue | +L |
| Dwarf vs. troll/giant | +1 |
| Halfling vs. troll/giant/ogre | +L |

**A roll of 1 always fails.** A 6 is not an auto-success but explodes.

## Who gets attacked · 4AD 96–97

| Situation | Attacks |
|---|---|
| Room, Foes < PCs | each a different PC; **you** choose who is spared |
| Room, equal | one each |
| Room, Foes > PCs | spread evenly, you assign extras — **except Hated classes** |
| **Corridor** | **max 2 Foes**, hitting positions 1–2 |
| Wandering Monsters in corridor | 2 of them hit positions **3–4** |

**Hatreds:** trolls/goblins/kobolds → dwarves · orcs → elves · undead → clerics.
**Outnumbered? Back into a corridor.**

## XP · 4AD 117

| Source | XP |
|---|---|
| 10 Minor Foe **encounters** | 1 |
| Secret revealed (3 Clues) | 1 |
| Each Major Foe | 1 |
| Final Boss | +1 |

Slower options: **4AD 117–118**. Or cap at 1 Level per adventure.

## Conditions

| | Rule | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | Once per adventure. Cleared room, adjacent tiles clear, doors nailed or **wedged**. +1 Life or 1 ability each, then 1-in-6 WM | **4AD 114** |

# 7 · ELEVEN RULES OF THUMB

1. Set the tile or time limit **before** you start.
2. Carry spare torches — no light is **−2 to everything** and no Searching.
3. Door wedges (5gp, reusable) beat nails (4gp, single use).
4. Outnumbered → fight in a **corridor**. 4 Foes become 2 attacks.
5. Don't backtrack casually — every re-entered tile rolls on a table with a Boss on it.
6. Attack **one PC at a time**, watching each result.
7. Reaction rolls are a gamble: a Fight result means they swing first.
8. Search every safe empty tile. Clues are XP and keys; secret doors **double** treasure.
9. Remember the hatreds — a dwarf in a goblin dungeon soaks every spare attack.
10. Fleeing works: **HCL locks on first contact**, so a hard room stays that L when you return.
11. Budget the walk home. The Boss is not the last fight.
